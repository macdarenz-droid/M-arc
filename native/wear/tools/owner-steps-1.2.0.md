New phone test app: https://github.com/macdarenz-droid/M-arc/actions/runs/39030388978/artifacts/12100984314 (download MARC-DEBUG-APK, run 2113). Watch app 1.2.0 attached (private: it carries your name and your watch's ID).

What changed: the new watch screens (Set, Up next, Rest, Phone not connected, Saved). In Up next you pick the next exercise on the watch. The phone side also fixes the set times from your last run.

Safety first: use a practice workout, not a real one. If M/ARC Watch closes by itself even once, stop and tell me the step and the screen. I'll send a safe build.

Before you start
1. M/ARC: Settings, Export backup.
2. Install this APK over your current M/ARC. Don't uninstall.
3. Install watch 1.2.0 over 1.1.2 with DevEco Assistant.
4. M/ARC: Settings, turn on "Start rest after each set".
5. Start a practice workout with at least 3 weight exercises of 4 or more sets each (no bodyweight or timed ones).
6. M/ARC: Settings, tap Version 7 times, Watch lab, Connect. Then open M/ARC Watch.

E1. Screens. A photo of each: Set; Up next (swipe up or down, or tap the exercise name); Rest (after a set). Say if any text is cut off at the round edge or too small, and if the clock at the top matches your phone.
E2. Sets. After each Complete set, Rest shows "Set N saved" within 3 s. Tap +30 s once and Skip once: the phone's rest grows by 30 s, then ends. Screenshot of Watch lab with the "mw cmd" lines.
E3. Up next. With no rest running (tap Skip), open Up next and tap another exercise with 2 or more sets left, then Complete set. The phone logs that exercise's set, and the watch stays on it for the next set. Then open a different exercise card on the phone: the watch follows it.
E4. Offline. Phone Bluetooth off, tap Complete set. Within 30 s the watch shows Phone not connected, "1 action", "Pending". Bluetooth on, wait until Huawei Health shows the watch connected, then tap Connect in Watch lab. Film the watch from tapping Connect until Set or Rest is back: "Phone connected / Saved" shows for about 3 s.
E5. Late start. Close M/ARC Watch, lock the phone and wait 5 minutes. Open M/ARC Watch first, wait 15 s, then open M/ARC. Do 5 Completes. Screenshot of Watch lab, scrolled down to the line "! native gap up ...s". If that number is under 60 or the line is missing, do E5 again.

Across the steps, log at least 12 sets with rests, so we see the watch holds up for a whole workout.

Then tap Finish and press and hold "Hold to discard"; don't tap the save buttons. Photo of the watch: "Start your workout". Turn "Start rest after each set" off again if it was off. Send everything with its step name.
