New phone test app: the MARC-DEBUG-APK artifact of the M/ARC gate run on this commit (link in the owner's message). Watch app 1.4.0 attached (private: it carries your name and your watch's ID).

What changed on the watch:
- A heart next to your heart rate at the top; it beats at your heart rate.
- Rest: -15 s, +15 s and Skip, like the phone.
- Adjust: kg (or lb) by 1, a DECIMALS field (.00, .25, .50, .75, so 15.5 kg works), then reps; swipe left or right between them.
- After 15 s without a tap on Set or Rest, the screen goes dark grey; a tap brings it back. Your brightness setting is not touched.
- Finish the workout on the phone: a short "Workout done" animation, two buzzes, then your highlights (PRs, time and sets, kg lifted, exercises, peak and average heart rate). A tap goes back.
- The "Phone not connected" title now shows (it never drew before).

Safety first: use a practice workout, not a real one. If M/ARC Watch closes by itself even once, stop and tell me the step and the screen, and put 1.3.0 back with DevEco Assistant.

Before you start
1. M/ARC: Settings, Export backup.
2. Install this APK over your current M/ARC. Don't uninstall.
3. Install watch 1.4.0 over 1.3.0 with DevEco Assistant.
4. M/ARC: Settings, turn on "Start rest after each set".
5. Start a practice workout with at least 2 weight exercises of 4 or more sets each, one you have done before (for a PR).
6. M/ARC: Settings, tap Version 7 times, Watch lab, Connect. Then open M/ARC Watch.

G1. Heart. The heart and your bpm show at the top and the heart beats; walk or do a set and it beats faster. Photo of the top of the screen.
G2. Rest. During a rest tap -15 s, then +15 s: the phone's rest moves the same way. Then Skip.
G3. Adjust. Tap the kg value: + three times (it goes up by 1 each time), swipe right to DECIMALS and tap + twice (.50), Done, Complete set. The phone saves that weight (for example 63.5 kg).
G4. Dark screen. Leave the watch on Rest for 15 s without touching it: it goes dark grey. Tap it: back to normal. Then lower your arm until the screen goes off and raise it again: say whether it comes back bright or grey.
G5. Done. Finish the workout on the phone (the real Finish, not discard): the watch shows the animation, buzzes twice, then the highlights. Photo of the highlights. Tap: back to the start screen.
G6. Whole workout. Log at least 12 sets with rests from the watch, no crash.

Then delete the practice workout from History on the phone if you don't want to keep it. Turn "Start rest after each set" off again if it was off. Send everything with its step name.
