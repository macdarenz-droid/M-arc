Your data

Your workouts stay on your phone. Two optional features send data, and only after you turn them on: the online coach, Escobar, and anonymous error reports. There is no account.

Summary Full policy

Summary

What you log is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. M/ARC's own server keeps no copy of your workouts; with the coach on, Anthropic may keep a request for up to 30 days (see the full policy). If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android can copy the app's data to your Google account; Google runs that backup, and you can turn it off there. If this summary and the full policy below ever differ, the full policy applies.

Outgoing data

Nothing leaves the phone until you turn on Online coach (Escobar) or error reports. With either on, these are the main points; the full policy below lists every item.

  1. With Online coach on, your message, the conversation so far, a short summary built on the phone and the workout history the coach asks for travel through the project's own server to the AI that answers. Numbers in the answer are checked against your log, and a sentence that cannot be checked is marked.

  2. Health data leaves only while Share health data is on, and body data only while Share body data is on. One exception: the readiness score and muscle recovery, worked out on the phone, are sent whenever the coach is on; the numbers behind them are not.

  3. A photo you attach is sent once, with that message, shrunk to at most 900 pixels.

  4. Turn a switch off and that data is no longer sent again from the coach's earlier lookups.

  5. With the coach on, the app also sends a random device id and the app version, and the server sees your IP address. While the app is open, it may check whether the coach server is reachable; that check sends no data.

  6. If you set your own coach server in Settings, coach requests go to that server instead, under its operator's terms.

  7. Tap Report under a coach reply and pick a reason, and the text of that reply, the reason and the app version go to the project's own server, even if you set your own coach server. They are kept 90 days, with no device id.

  8. With error reports on, a short technical report is sent when something breaks. It never holds workouts, health or body data, conversations or settings.

Coach quotas are counted per device and per IP address. There is no account to count against.

Sharing switches

Share health data

Share health data: sleep, resting heart rate, session heart rate. Share body data: weight and measurements. Both are off until you turn them on, and both live in Settings under Escobar once Online coach is on.

Backup and export

Export backup writes a JSON file you keep wherever you like. Restore backup asks you to confirm, then applies, and gives you an Undo. Export CSV hands you every filled-in set, for the last 90 days or all time. On Android, a weekly backup reminder is there if you want it. If saved data ever fails to read when the app starts, it is kept aside, not thrown away: Settings shows it under Unreadable data kept aside, with Save rescue file.

Offline

The app opens without a network: the Android app carries its own files, and the web version keeps a copy of every built file. The coach notes, weekly review and lighter-week suggestion run on the phone. Only a conversation with Escobar needs a connection.

Website

This site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and loads no analytics. Every font, image and script comes from this site's own address. The Copy buttons write to your clipboard only when you tap them. The host that serves these files keeps its own access logs, as every web host does; the site adds nothing to them.
