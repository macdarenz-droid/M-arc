Set it up

  1. 1

    Set the seat so your knees line up with the pivot.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

Risks and when to stop

  • Too much weight turns the curl into a swing.

Knee pain

Grip

Single handles, palms facing each other as the hands meet. Hold the wrist at the same angle all rep.

Common handling mistakes

  • Bending elbows into a press

    Fix: Keep the elbow bend fixed. Move at the shoulders.

  • Swinging with too much weight

    Fix: Use a weight you can move slowly with a soft elbow bend.

  • Arms opening too far back

    Fix: Stop when the hands are in line with the chest.

Risks and when to stop

Grip

Palms flat, about shoulder-width. Push through the heel and outer edge of the palm.

Common handling mistakes

  • Hips sagging, low back arching

    Fix: Brace your abs, squeeze your glutes, tuck your pelvis slightly.

  • Hands not under the shoulders

    Fix: Stack your shoulders over your hands.

  • Dropping fast to the floor

    Fix: Lower under control and touch the floor lightly.

The belly holds the body straight, much like a plank.

If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansFlared elbows can load the shoulder more.

    FixTurn your hands forward and keep your elbows close.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Kneel, palms flat on the floor about shoulder-width apart.

  2. 2

    Shift forward until your shoulders are over your hands.

  3. 3

    Straighten your legs, feet together, body in one line.

  4. 4

    Brace your trunk and tense your glutes and thighs.

  5. 5

    Squeeze the floor with your hands.

Risks and when to stop

  • Elbows kept close may ease stress on the shoulder.
  • A cluttered or slippery floor makes the set unsafe.
