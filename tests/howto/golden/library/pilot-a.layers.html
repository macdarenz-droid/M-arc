Grip

Palms forward, handle across the middle of your palm, thumb wrapped. Keep your wrist straight.

Keep the hand in line with the forearm, not bent up or down.

Common handling mistakes

  • Wrists bending up or down

    Fix: Keep the hand in line with the forearm.

  • Swinging the body

    Fix: Use a lighter dumbbell and lift with control.

  • Elbows drifting back or forward

    Fix: Keep the elbows at your sides. Never let them travel back.

Set it up

  4. 4

Risks and when to stop

  • Bent wrists under load can cause wrist discomfort.
  • Across weight training, dropped weights are the most common way people get badly hurt.

Wrist pain

Can't grip, wrist changed shape, or hand gone numb? Get it checked today.

Tingling, keeps coming back, or no better after two weeks' rest? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.

Grip

Hold each rope end, hands against the knobs. Palms face each other, wrists straight.

Fist curling toward the forearm? Keep the wrist straight.

Common handling mistakes

  • Leaning over or arching back

    Fix: Stand taller. Tilt only from the hips.

  • Elbows drifting forward or out

    Fix: Keep your elbows at your sides.

  • Rope rising too high

    Fix: Stop when your forearms are just past parallel.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this in the back of your upper arms.

If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansThe rope rises higher than just above parallel.

    FixStop when your forearms are just past parallel.

  • Usually meansYou lean too far forward or arch your back.

    FixStop and don't train through the pain. Stand taller, tilt only from the hips.

  • Usually meansHeavy, low-rep sets feel uncomfortable in the elbows for many.

    FixStop and don't train through the pain. Go lighter, don't lock out your elbows.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Fix the rope to the pulley at its top position.

  2. 2

    Hold each end with your hand against the knob, thumbs wrapped.

  3. 3

    Stand facing the machine, feet hip-width or staggered, torso tall.

  4. 4

    Elbows at your sides, forearms just past parallel, shoulders down and back.

  5. 5

    Pick a weight you can move with control.

Risks and when to stop

  • Locking the elbows out hard at the bottom can injure the elbows.
  • Leaning or arching strains the lower back.
  • Too much weight moved with momentum can lead to injury.

Elbow pain

Severe pain and hard to move, heard a snap, changed shape, tingling, or numbness? Get it checked today.

No better after a few weeks? See a doctor.
