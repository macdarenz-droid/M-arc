Grip

Hook the bar at the base of your fingers, wrist straight. Pull with your elbows so your back does more.

Wrist curling, or bar slipping to your fingertips? Finish the rep and end the set.

Sore wrist? Use the neutral-grip handles.

Common handling mistakes

  • Pulling behind your neck

    Fix: Pull in front of your face, to your upper chest.

  • Curling wrists, squeezing hard

    Fix: Straighten your wrist. Grip only as hard as needed.

  • Leaning back and swinging

    Fix: Hold a small lean still and go lighter.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this in the sides of your back, under your armpits. Drive your elbows down.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Snug the knee pad so you can't lift off.

  2. 2

    Push the pin fully in. Start light.

  3. 3

    Hands about 1.5 times shoulder width, palms away, thumb wrapped.

  4. 4

    Lean back slightly, about 30 degrees at most.

  5. 5

    Shoulders down, away from your ears. Then pull.

Risks and when to stop

  • Behind-the-neck pulling is linked to a looser front shoulder.
  • Rocking uses your lower back and jerks your shoulders.
  • Pulling past where your elbows stop stresses the shoulder joint.

Wrist pain

Can't grip, wrist changed shape, or hand gone numb? Get it checked today.

Tingling, keeps coming back, or no better after two weeks' rest? See a doctor.

Shoulder pain

Sudden or severe, after falling, or arm won't move? Get it checked today.

Getting worse, very hard to move, or no better after two weeks? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.

Grip

Pull with your elbows, not your hands. Handle at the base of your fingers, thumbs wrapped, wrists straight.

Curling wrists mean your grip is taking over. Finish the rep, then end the set.

Common handling mistakes

  • Rocking your torso

    Fix: Pick a weight you can pull with a still torso.

  • Rounding your lower back

    Fix: Arms go long, back stays tall.

  • Shrugging and flaring elbows

    Fix: Shoulders down, elbows tucked to your ribs.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this between your shoulder blades and down the sides of your back. Lead with your elbows.

MainAlso workingShould not take overWhere it may hurt
If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansYou're shrugging, or your elbows flare up.

    FixKeep shoulders down, elbows low and close. Pull to your belly.

  • Usually meansYou're rocking to move the weight, or rounding at the front.

    FixGo lighter and sit tall. Only your arms and shoulder blades move.

  • Usually meansYour elbows go too far back and your shoulders roll forward.

    FixStop once elbows pass your sides, chest up. If light weight still pinches, stop rowing.

  • Usually meansWrists curling or bending to the little finger, or sore from pressing.

    FixKeep your wrist straight. If it's sore, go lighter and stop if it hurts.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Clip on the V-handle. Start light.

  2. 2

    Sit facing the stack, feet flat on the plate.

  3. 3

    Bend your knees, not your back, to take the handle. Thumbs wrapped.

  4. 4

    Sit tall, knees soft. Push back until the weight clears the stack.

  5. 5

    Chest up, shoulders down before you pull. It's hardest at the start.

Risks and when to stop

  • Rocking loads your lower back. Repeated rounding under load hurt discs in lab tests.
  • Shrugging loads your neck. Elbows too far back can pinch your shoulder.
  • Curled wrists tire your forearms first and can make wrists sore over time.

Tingling, keeps coming back, or no better after two weeks' rest? See a doctor.

Getting worse, very hard to move, or no better after two weeks? See a doctor.
