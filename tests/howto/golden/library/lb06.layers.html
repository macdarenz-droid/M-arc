Grip

Hold the handles in both hands. Start the crunch with your abs, not your arms.

Common handling mistakes

  • Pulling with the arms

    Fix: Start the crunch with your abs.

  • Using too much weight

    Fix: Lower the weight to protect your back.

  • Letting the stack rest

    Fix: Keep tension. Return without letting the stack rest.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Set the seat to a comfortable height and choose the weight.

  2. 2

    Sit with your hips pressed against the back of the seat.

  3. 3

    Push your shins against the padded roller and tighten your abs.

  4. 4

    Hold the handles in both hands.

  5. 5

    Breathe out as you crunch. Don't strain your neck.

Risks and when to stop

  • Too much weight can strain the back.
  • Lower back pain? This machine works the hip flexors hard, so take care.

Back pain

Pain, numbness, tingling or weakness in both legs? Numb genitals or anus? Bladder, bowel or sexual changes? Chest pain? Serious accident? Get emergency help now. Sudden severe or fast-worsening pain? Hot, cold, shivery or unwell? See a doctor today.

Grip

Hold the handles, pads just above the elbows. Lead with the elbows.

Keep your wrists neutral.

Common handling mistakes

  • Swinging the body up

    Fix: Lower the weight and lift without momentum.

  • Shrugging the shoulders

    Fix: Keep your shoulders down. Go lighter if needed.

  • Too heavy for shoulder height

    Fix: Lower the weight.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this in the sides of your shoulders.

MainAlso workingShould not take overWhere it may hurt
If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansShrugging lets the traps dominate.

    FixKeep your shoulders down. Go lighter.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Set the seat so your shoulders line up with the machine's pivots.

  2. 2

    Sit with your back on the pad, feet flat on the floor.

  3. 3

    Arms on the pads, just above the elbows. Hold the handles.

  4. 4

    Pick a lighter weight than you think.

  5. 5

    Lower slowly, stopping before the stack rests.

Risks and when to stop

  • Swinging the body to finish a rep can lead to injury.
  • It is easy to choose a weight that is too heavy.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.
