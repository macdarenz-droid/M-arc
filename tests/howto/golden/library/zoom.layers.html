Grip

Common handling mistakes

  • Locking the knees

    Fix: Stop just short of straight. Keep a small bend.

  • Hips lifting, lower back rounding

    Fix: Set the seat so your knees start at about 90 degrees.

  • Knees falling in

    Fix: Keep your kneecaps in line with your feet.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this in the front of your thighs and your glutes.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Sit back, back and tailbone flat against the pad.

  2. 2

    Feet flat on the plate, toes forward.

  3. 3

    Set the seat: knees at about 90 degrees, thighs clear of ribs.

  4. 4

    Hold the handles lightly and brace your stomach.

  5. 5

    Breathe out as you push. Control the plate on the way back.

Risks and when to stop

  • Locking the knees under load is very rarely linked to knee dislocation.
  • Heavy presses to failure sent blood pressure very high, partly from breath holding.
  • Avoid rounding your lower back off the pad.

Knee pain

Severe pain, badly swollen, changed shape, locking, giving way, or can't put weight on it? Get it checked today.

No better after a few weeks? See a doctor.

Back pain

Pain, numbness, tingling or weakness in both legs? Numb genitals or anus? Bladder, bowel or sexual changes? Chest pain? Serious accident? Get emergency help now. Sudden severe or fast-worsening pain? Hot, cold, shivery or unwell? See a doctor today.

No better in a few weeks? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.
