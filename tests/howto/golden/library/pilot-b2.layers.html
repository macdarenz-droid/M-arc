If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansYour hip flexors are helping.

    FixSlow down. Keep your low back on the mat.

  • Usually meansYour neck or arms are pulling.

    FixSet your chin once. Cross your arms.

  • Usually meansPelvis tipping forward from a fast, hip-led curl.

    FixSlow down. Keep your low back on the mat.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart.

  2. 2

    Arms crossed on your chest, or fingertips light by your ears.

  3. 3

    Tuck your chin slightly and keep it there.

Risks and when to stop

  • A fast, hip-led crunch adds stress on the low back.

Back pain

Pain, numbness, tingling or weakness in both legs? Numb genitals or anus? Bladder, bowel or sexual changes? Chest pain? Serious accident? Get emergency help now. Sudden severe or fast-worsening pain? Hot, cold, shivery or unwell? See a doctor today.

No better in a few weeks? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.
