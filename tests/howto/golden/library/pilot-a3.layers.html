Set it up

  1. 1

    Dumbbells at your sides, feet about hip to shoulder width apart.

  2. 2

    Step one foot back, a full-length step.

  3. 3

    Lower the back knee slowly toward the floor, chest up.

  4. 4

    Press the front foot down, swing the back leg forward to stand.

  5. 5

    Alternate legs, or finish all reps on one side before switching.

Risks and when to stop

  • Too heavy a load costs the upright trunk, the controlled descent and balance.
  • Crashing into the bottom position instead of lowering under control.

Knee pain

Severe pain, badly swollen, changed shape, locking, giving way, or can't put weight on it? Get it checked today.

No better after a few weeks? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.

Grip

Thumbs wrapped, palms facing each other. Keep your wrists neutral.

Keep the wrists neutral, not bent forward or back.

Common handling mistakes

  • Wrists bending forward or back

    Fix: Keep your wrists neutral.

  • Going below chest level

    Fix: Stop level with the shoulders or chest.

  • Elbows bending more going down

    Fix: Keep the same slight bend.

  2. 2

    Press up to shoulder-width, thumbs wrapped. Palms in, elbows slightly bent.

  5. 5

Risks and when to stop

  • Shoulder injuries are common in lifting and linked to poor technique.
  • Dropped weights are the most common way lifters get badly hurt.

Shoulder pain

Sudden or severe, after falling, or arm won't move? Get it checked today.

Getting worse, very hard to move, or no better after two weeks? See a doctor.
