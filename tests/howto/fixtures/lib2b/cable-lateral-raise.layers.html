Grip

Hook the bar where your fingers start, like carrying a bag. Wrap your thumb: you hang from the bar.

Bar sliding to your fingertips? End the set.

Common handling mistakes

  • Bouncing at the bottom

    Fix: Lower slowly to straight arms.

  • Grip too wide

    Fix: Hands just outside your shoulders.

  • Bar slipping to fingertips

    Fix: Set the bar where your fingers start.

Where you should feel it

You should feel this in the sides of your back. Drive your elbows to your ribs.

MainAlso workingShould not take overWhere it may hurt
If you feel it in…

  • Usually meansYou're shrugging toward your ears.

    FixSet your shoulders down before your arms bend.

  • Usually meansGrip too wide, or your chin reaching for the bar.

    FixBring your hands in, head level. If it keeps hurting, stop pull-ups.

  • Usually meansToo many sets, or a grip your elbows dislike.

    FixTry neutral handles or a shoulder-width grip, and fewer sets.

  • Usually meansBar in your fingertips, wrist curled, or an old injury.

    FixKeep your wrist level. If it's sore, use neutral handles and stop if it hurts.

Set it up

  1. 1

    Bar fixed and dry? Chalk sweaty hands.

  2. 2

    Can't do a few clean reps? Use a band or assisted machine.

  3. 3

    Hands just outside your shoulders, palms away.

  4. 4

    Hang still, legs together.

  5. 5

    Arms straight, shoulders down. Then pull.

Risks and when to stop

  • Dropping into the bottom jerks your weight through the shoulder.
  • Wide grips are linked to shoulder pinching.
  • Chin reaching strains your neck. Fingertip grips can slip off.

Wrist pain

Can't grip, wrist changed shape, or hand gone numb? Get it checked today.

Tingling, keeps coming back, or no better after two weeks' rest? See a doctor.

Shoulder pain

Sudden or severe, after falling, or arm won't move? Get it checked today.

Getting worse, very hard to move, or no better after two weeks? See a doctor.

Elbow pain

Severe pain and hard to move, heard a snap, changed shape, tingling, or numbness? Get it checked today.

No better after a few weeks? See a doctor.

General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.
