Grip

Overhand full grip, hands about shoulder-width, thumbs wrapped.

Keep the wrists in line with the forearms.

Common handling mistakes

  • Leaning over or arching back

    Fix: Stand taller. Tilt only from the hips.

  • Elbows drifting forward

    Fix: Keep the elbows at your sides. Stop the return at upper-chest level.

  • Too heavy, moved with momentum

    Fix: Go lighter and move with control.

Setup

  1. 1

    Set the pulley at the top position and attach a straight bar.

  2. 2

    Grip the bar overhand, hands shoulder-width, thumbs wrapped.

  3. 3

    Stand facing the machine, feet hip- to shoulder-width or staggered, core braced.

  4. 4

    Bar at upper chest, elbows at sides, shoulders down and back.

  5. 5

    Press down to straight arms, hips tilted slightly so the bar clears.

Risks

  • Leaning or arching can strain the lower back.
  • Heavy loads feel uncomfortable in the elbows for many people.
  • Too much weight moved with momentum can lead to injury.
