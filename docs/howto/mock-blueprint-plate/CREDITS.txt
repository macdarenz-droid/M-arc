Technical Plate (blueprint-plate) mockups: credits and licences

1. The plate itself: no photo, video or stock art. The figure, the positions, the hand path and the angle arc are
   computed by src/plate.mjs (forward kinematics to SVG) written for this mockup.
   - Body proportions: segment lengths as fractions of height from D. A. Winter, Biomechanics and Motor Control of
     Human Movement (4th ed., Wiley 2009), anthropometry chapter (upper arm .186, forearm .146, hand .108,
     thigh .245, shank .246, shoulder width .259, hip width .191). Numbers only; no figure was copied.
   - Movement and cues: the M/ARC repo's own files src/formguide/exercises/lib_dumbbell_lateral_raise.ts and
     src/formguide/research/lib_dumbbell_lateral_raise.json (ACE Lateral Raise; PMC7503819; PMC12277279).
   None of the free assets from tonight's research list was used, so no third-party imagery needs a licence here.

2. Typeface: Inter Variable (latin wght woff2) from node_modules/@fontsource-variable/inter.
   SIL Open Font License 1.1, the same file the app ships.

3. Body map paths: src/svg/bodyMuscles.ts in the M/ARC repo, generated from the "body-muscles" package by Ivan Vulovic.
   Apache License 2.0; see the repo's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

4. Escobar mark: src/assets/escobar-mark.png from the M/ARC repo (the owner's own logo).

5. Theme colours: src/theme/themes.ts (bundled to src/themes.mjs with esbuild, themeToCss) and the tokens block
   of src/ui/styles.css, read from the main worktree at 578bbc8.

6. Icons: redrawn from the app's own src/ui/icons.tsx. Two new glyphs were drawn for this mockup: "angle"
   (the How to do it pill) and "trace". The status-bar glyphs are generic shapes. No brand logos were used or invented.

7. Changes after the judge's review (2026-09-29), all in src/plate.mjs and src/build.mjs
   - Body: a skull, jaw and chin (head .13H) narrower at the jaw so a neck shows; a concave trapezius slope from the neck
     to the acromion; feet about hip-width with thighs that taper, so the legs separate below the crotch.
   - Dumbbells: in a front view a dumbbell is seen end-on (its handle points at the camera at both the start and the top),
     so it is drawn as a hex head with the handle end-cap, not as a round ring. The judge asked for "handle plus two plates";
     that side-on view is not what a camera in front would see, so it was not drawn.
   - The dotted shoulder-height line now runs from the plate edge to the shoulder joint, under the figure, so the raised
     hand meets it.
   - f-trace.mp4 shows only the cited Lift cue ("The elbows lead, the hands follow."); the fourth, uncited cue is gone.
   - Tempo labels keep at least 6 px apart at 320 px width.
   It is still a drawn figure. Tracing the outline from a real athlete photo (potrace) remains the production plan.
