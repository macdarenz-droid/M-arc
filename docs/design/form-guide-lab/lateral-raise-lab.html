Lateral Raise Lab

The dumbbell lateral raise, rebuilt from scratch. You get a 3D figure you can turn to any angle. The muscles visibly shorten as they work, and the load curve comes from real physics. The common mistake plays right next to the correct rep.

    What changes

    The version being built now draws each exercise as a flat picture from one fixed side. This one keeps a single moving skeleton in 3D. Each exercise becomes a short motion description (joint angles over time), and everything else is computed from it: every camera angle, how long each muscle is, and the load at each moment.

    Why these choices

    1. Watching the right rep and the common mistake together teaches better than watching the right rep alone. The mistake shows what to avoid. BMC Neuroscience, 2021
    2. Beginners learn more from a demo when they are told first what to watch. That is the "Watch" line above the figure. Frontiers in Psychology, 2016
    3. Mirrored demos make you flip left and right in your head. Being able to turn a 3D figure helps people read the pose. That is why there is a "Your view" camera behind the figure, facing the way you face. Virtual Reality, 2026
    4. For learning body movements, animations can beat still pictures, so motion stays the main mode and still frames are the fallback. Wong et al.
    5. The lateral raise itself:
      • Raise slightly in front of the body (the scapular plane).
      • Don't turn the thumbs down: rotating the upper arm inward raises upper-trap work (EMG study, 2020).
      • A dumbbell's load climbs to shoulder height and is lightest at the bottom (dumbbell vs cable study).
      The model uses all three.

    What it would take

    • It replaces the flat rig in the current build (GU-7a). The player, sheet, controls and muscle names being built now all carry over.
    • It needs no library. It is one small drawing engine plus about a screen of numbers per exercise, and it works offline.
    • Needs checking on a real phone: smoothness, battery, and whether the plain mannequin reads as well as the detailed figure.
    • Where each muscle attaches has to be checked for every new muscle. This sample does two: the side delts and the upper traps.
