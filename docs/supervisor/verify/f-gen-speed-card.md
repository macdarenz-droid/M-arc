## Goal
- Make the render, and so lib7b's 7b-A2, fast enough that **7b-A2 is ≤ 300 s in every CI job** (D-GCS-BUDGET2).
- **Output stays byte-identical:** every generated file, built page and golden pin is unchanged, proven by a full before/after byte compare (G2, G3).
- Never raise a timeout, skip or weaken a check, or narrow what a render covers.

## What a render costs now

- `plates.mjs` is 94 % of a render on both trees, as #437's PLAN found (311.4 of 333.0 s on its box).
- 7b-A2's tree setup (`pairTree` + `pageOf` 5.7 s, `keyTree` 2.2 s) is small. So 7b-A2 is one full render of a tree with 3 more rows: **1.09×** a repo render on an idle box.

**`plates.mjs` alone** (`prof.mjs`, three base runs: 362.1, 364.6 and 362.6 s):
- 362 s of wall time, but only 192-197 s of parent CPU.
- It starts **205 child node processes, strictly one after another**: at most 1 runs at any time, and the parent waits for each.
- The parent's CPU profile is 53 % idle (184.9 of 346.5 s sampled). That is #437's "about half a CPU".

## Options weighed (each measured on `plates.mjs` alone, same box)

| option | where | wall s | change | output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | | 362 | | sha `88ac0f60…` |
| **A. Overlap PQ-H7's builds** (recommended first) | `qa/node.mjs` h7 only | **280.8** | −22 % | sha `88ac0f60…`: **byte-identical** |
| **A+B. Also start the 15 batch pages at once** | + `gen/plates.mjs` `libraryOutputs` | **216.9** | −40 % | body sha `62213d5e…` equals base's. Headers move, because `gen/plates.mjs` is a writer of 100 files |

- **A. Start the row's two PQ-H7 process builds first, both at once, then do the in-process renders, then read the results in their old order.**
  - The experiment started them at the top of `h7` and kept every problem key and text.
  - Children ran 2 at a time; the parent CPU was about the same (199.7 s, against 192-197 s). The child wall sum rose from 197 to 233 s, because each child now shares the CPU.
  - Why it keeps the check: the two builds are still two separate processes, each importing the spec afresh, and the bytes are still compared. Only when they start changes.
  - `qa/node.mjs` is in no plugin's input list (gen/*.mjs read) and in none of the 134 row entries of `tools/plates/library/MANIFEST.json`. So **no generated byte, header or pin moves** (measured for `plates.mjs`'s 100 outputs, headers included; G4 proves the rest), and it needs no slot between waves.
- **B. Prefetch the batch pages.** At the top of `libraryOutputs` (`gen/plates.mjs:181-200`), start `platesPage` for every shipped row's page, so the 15 page children run while QA runs.
  - The experiment started all 15 at once, unbounded. The child wall sum was 670.6 s, because they ran concurrently.
  - `gen/plates.mjs` is hashed into every header it writes (`lib/inputs.mjs:31`): the 98 `ht-*.ts`, `generated/index.ts` and `plate.css`.
  - So B moves line 1 and the `hashes:` line of those files: header churn of the F-GEN-HELD-CLEAN kind. The bodies stay byte-identical, measured by a sha that leaves out those two lines. Q-GSP-3.
  - No byte-safe place for B exists:
    - `batch-golden.mjs`, `build-page.mjs`, `plates-page.mjs`, `golden.mjs`, `zoom/inline.mjs` and `qa/engine.mjs` are header inputs or MANIFEST-pinned.
    - A prefetch hidden in `candidate.mjs` would build all 15 pages for any caller of `candidateOf`, tests included. Rejected.
- **C. QA in worker threads, rows in a pool: not now.** After A+B, the parent's own CPU (172-200 s here) is the floor. Breaking it moves R, `again` and fresh into other isolates, which changes what "the same process" means for those checks. That is a larger design and review. Not measured.
- **D. A cache of QA results across processes or trees: rejected.** The 7b-A2 render would not run PQ-H7 and the other checks for the 90 rows it shares with the repo. That narrows what a render covers. The in-process `qaCache` (`gen/plates.mjs:152-168`) stays as it is.
- **E. A faster engine, specs or `applyZooms`: never here.** They are MANIFEST-locked or render inputs (above).
- **F. Raise the 600 s limits: rejected** (D-GCS-NORAISE).
- **G. Give lib7b its own CI job or shard: out of scope.** That is `.github/**` (supervisor) and GATE-SPLIT's area. It is the supervisor's fallback under Stop 1.

**What A and B may give on CI [est].**
- `plates.mjs` is 94 % of a render, so A is about −21 % and A+B about −38 % of one render on an idle box.
- If that held on CI, 7b-A2 would go from 350-506 s to about 277-400 s with A, and to about 217-314 s with A+B.
- But CI runs 7b-A2 while vitest's other workers use the CPUs. Overlap helps only when a CPU is free. So the real gain on CI is not known until the PLAN's probe run.
- **A alone very likely misses 300 s in the slowest job.** A+B is borderline there.

## The change

1. **A, in `tools/plates/library/qa/node.mjs` only** (`h7` :217-229, `h7Fresh` :231-245, `h7Builds` :247-250):
   - At the top of `h7`, when `R` is set, the mode is not `'approved'`, and `c.freshSpec` and `c.rebuild` are functions (today's conditions for the builds, except that the fresh render's outcome is not known yet), call `c.rebuild()` twice. Turn each promise into a settled record (`{ v }` or `{ e }`), so neither can reject unhandled.
   - `h7Fresh` takes the two records instead of calling `h7Builds`. After the fresh render and its comparison, it reads them in today's order:
     1. a's error;
     2. a's `'null'` (`the rebuilt page has no plate for the id`);
     3. b's error;
     4. b's `'null'`;
     5. `a !== b` gives `H7.determinism:build`.
   - Every throw lands in the existing `catch` as `H7.determinism:fresh-threw`, with today's message text.
   - If the fresh import or render throws first, the `catch` reports the fresh error, exactly as today, and awaits both records before it returns, so no child outlives the row.
   - `h7Builds` stays exported, unchanged (tests use it).
   - `h10` is untouched (Q-GSP-8).
2. **B, only if Q-GSP-3 rules it in:**
   - in `gen/plates.mjs` `libraryOutputs` (:181-200), before the row loop, start `platesPage(row, all)` for each shipped row through a bounded pool (Q-GSP-5). Attach a no-op `catch` to each prefetch: the loop's own `await` still gets the same memoised promise and its error;
   - then one `[golden update]` regenerate commit for the header lines, in the D-PFS-HEADERS form.
3. **New `tests/howto/f-gen-speed.test.ts`** (fast, stubs only; no render). It pins PQ-H7's results and messages for every path, and that both builds are in flight at once:
   - builds equal;
   - builds differ;
   - a throws;
   - a gives `'null'`;
   - b throws;
   - b gives `'null'`;
   - the fresh import throws while the builds are pending.

## Risks
- **The gain on CI is smaller than on an idle box.** Overlap needs a free CPU, and vitest's other workers use them.
  - Mitigation: the PLAN probe measures A and A+B on CI before anything is built for keeps; Stop 1.
- **More processes at once slow other tests.** A runs 2 children per row, and B up to the pool size.
  - Mitigation: G8's +10 % bar on every ≥ 100 s test; Q-GSP-5's bound; Stop 4.
- **Memory.** Peak RSS with 2 children (A), or up to 15 (the unbounded B experiment), was not measured.
  - Mitigation: post the peak RSS of one render (`/usr/bin/time -v`) at PLAN, for base, A and A+B.
- **Changed error paths in PQ-H7.** Today the second build never starts when the first throws or gives `'null'`; under A, both start.
  - Mitigation: the reports and messages are pinned on base for every path (the new test, G5); m3, m4 and m7.
- **A weaker determinism check.** It stays two separate processes, each importing the spec afresh, compared byte for byte; only the start time moves.
  - Mitigation: m1 against `library-qa-h7.test.ts`'s composer case.
- **Header churn, if B is ruled.** About 100 headers move [from code; not run], which collides with F-GEN-HELD-CLEAN, #436 and 6d.
  - Mitigation: B starts after GHC; regenerate-only merges; never during a READY review.
- **Growth.** Each wave adds rows: about 3.7 s per plate today on this box (362 s for 98 plates), and about 2.9 s with A [derived].
  - Mitigation: G7's bar is re-measured on every proof run; the projection is posted at READY.
