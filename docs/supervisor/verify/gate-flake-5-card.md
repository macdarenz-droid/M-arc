Root cause, in short (what this card's research proved, and what it could not)
The three sightings are not one Paper defect. Every block involved runs Silent Black and Paper side by side (HT_FULL, harness.mjs:572, for HT-8's reduced and early-chip runs; HT-9 Show :7694; COPY-2 :8961; HT-8's themeRun also runs the other three themes), so Paper carries half the exposure, and nothing in the app or the gate takes a Paper-only path (index.html :19 and :218 set Paper's colours only; no Paper-only font or chunk). The COPY-2 repro below is red in both themes alike. Two mechanisms remain.
(A) COPY-2 (sighting 3) is proven: its snap rule (:8985) compares the panel's top between two consecutive requestAnimationFrame samples, but nothing bounds the time between them. When the page drops one frame, one sample pair holds two frames of the ease, and any two neighbouring frames among the measured ease's first five sum to more than the 25 px limit (12.7 + 15.6 = 28.4; 15.6 + 20.0 = 35.6; 20.0 + 19.9 = 39.9; 19.9 + 11.4 = 31.3), so one dropped frame in the first ~85 ms of the 320 ms ease is enough. The reviewer's 35.7 px at 103 ms is frames 2 and 3 in one sample. A 40 ms main-thread stall at the swap's second frame reproduced it 3 of 3 runs, 12 of 12 contexts, including the identical line. The animation itself is correct: plain runs step at most 20.1 px a frame.
(B) HT-8 and HT-9 Show (sightings 1 and 2) fit one failure seen through two code paths: a lazy How-to chunk, loaded for the first time in that context, does not arrive. The sheet (lazy.tsx:17), the exercise's ht-<id> chunk (HowToSheet.tsx:30) and Feel (Feel.tsx:48) all catch a failed chunk and show the toast 'Could not load the guide.', so HT-8 sees only an 8000 ms wait running out (`locator.waitFor: Timeout 8000ms exceeded.`; :8142 keeps only that first line and drops the call log that names the locator). Sighting 1's counts place it: Paper's themeRun stopped at How-to index 2 (pull-up), in openHowTo (the ht-pull-up chunk, harness.mjs:262) or at :8090 (feel-pull-up). The zoom host (ZoomHost.tsx:169 and :188, `void open(...)`) does not catch it, so HT-9 Show gets a page error that names the chunk. Both shapes were reproduced exactly by failing the chunk. Not verified: that sighting 1 was a failed chunk and not one later than 8 s (its log cannot tell them apart), and why a chunk failed in CI at all. On this machine every How-to chunk is served by the app's service worker from its cache (precached at install: 68 requests, 3.35 MB per context), and no request failed with 9 app contexts at once.
