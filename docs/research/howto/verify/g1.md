## 4. Open items for the supervisor

### 4.1 Equipment gaps (census `equipmentNeeds`)

**a. `lib_assisted_pull_up`** (census: assistMachine "compose", pullupBar, floor).
- **Missing:**
  - A mounting step or foot rails, which is how the lifter gets onto and off the knee pad.
  - A visible stack pin or assistance selector.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Card `setup[0]` depends on choosing the assistance (c4, c5).
  - The get-on/get-off route is the tier-A way-out gap: card `gaps[0]` says "no sourced way to get on and off the pad safely (including a knee slipping off the moving pad)".
  - The assistMachine composer is "kneePad on a platform + handles + stack", with no step.
- **Recommend:** add `mountStep` (or foot rails) and a `stackPin` marker to the assistMachine composer. Draw the step on the start pose only, as the way on and off, with no text claim until a source is found.

**b. `lib_shoulder_press`** (census: pivotArm "exists", seat, backPad, floor).
- **Missing:**
  - The existing pivotArm is the chest-press lever: a horizontal push, drawn side-on. The shoulder press needs a pivot position and arc that carry the handles up in front of or beside the head, visibly clear of the head and face (tier-A "path clear of face/neck").
  - The primitive draws a neutral grip, but the card draws the standard (wide) handles.
- **Card fields involved:**
  - `plate.variantLine`: "…the engine's pivot-arm primitive draws a neutral grip, so a wide-handle option is an equipment gap for the supervisor."
  - `gaps[1]`: "no source read describes the handle arc's clearance from the head or face".
  - `gaps[2]`: palm direction not sourced.
- **Recommend:**
  - Add an overhead-arc variant of pivotArm (`pivotArmOverhead`) with a head-clearance check in the screenshot gate.
  - Add a wide-handle option. Until then, draw the close handles (c7), which the card already supports.
  - Palm direction stays a drawing default, labelled as such, because no source gives one.

**c. `lib_chin_up`, `lib_hanging_knee_raise`:** no gap. The step or box is moved clear before the set, so the plate correctly omits it.

### 4.2 Unsourced safety points (tier A; recorded as gaps, not invented)

1. **Way out: how to fail a rep or get down safely.** No source was found on any of the 4 tier-A cards:
   - assisted_pull_up `gaps[0]`: "Tier A: no sourced way to get on and off the pad safely (including a knee slipping off the moving pad) or to leave the machine at the end of a set; left out, not invented."
   - chin_up `gaps[0]`: "Tier A: no sourced way to get down from the bar or fail a rep safely (dismount); left out, not invented."
   - hanging_knee_raise `gaps[0]`: "Tier A: no sourced way to get down from the bar safely; the parent's box-clear setup line is inherited, nothing new invented."
   - shoulder_press `gaps[0]` and `tierA.wayOut`: "no source read says how to fail a rep safely on a shoulder press machine".
   - **Recommend:** commission one fetcher task for a single shared tier-A "way out" source per equipment type (bar hang and dismount; assist machine; selectorized press), then add it as a claim on each card. Until then the sheets carry no way-out line, per content.md 5.2.
2. **Shoulder press handle clearance.** shoulder_press `gaps[1]`: "no source read describes the handle arc's clearance from the head or face." Recommend: solve it in the drawing (4.1b); it needs no text claim.
3. **Handle palm direction.**
   - shoulder_press `gaps[2]` and single_arm_lat_pulldown `grip.type`: "Neither technique source names a palm direction, so none is sourced here."
   - **Recommend:** you pick the drawing default (neutral for single-arm, matching the D-handle; close handles for the shoulder press) and record it as a drawing convention, not a claim.
4. **Load beyond body weight on chin_up.** chin_up `gaps[1]`: "how much load to add, and when, is not sourced." The order (more reps, less assistance, then added load) is sourced (c23) and used in `handlingMistakes[0].fix`. Recommend: leave it as is.
5. **What to do if the pain keeps coming back** (ruling R1):
   - hanging_knee_raise `gaps[2]` (lower back) and shoulder_press `gaps[3]` (sharp shoulder pinch) say no card source covers this.
   - The feel rows no longer carry an unsourced "stop and get it checked".
   - **Recommend:** confirm R1. The shared red-flag box plus the owner disclaimer ("General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.") cover it.
6. **Row wording versus 5.2 ("Rows say usually means").** Several rows now read "No source read links this feeling to a cause; the fault <source> warns about here is …", because a "usually means" clause would be invented.
   - Tier A: shoulder_press `feel.rows[0]`, `[1]`, `[2]`.
   - Tier B and C: all rows on horizontal_leg_press, incline_machine_press, chest_supported_row, straight_arm_pulldown, leg_press_calf_raise and resistance_band_row.
   - **Recommend a sheet-time rule:** a row without a sourced symptom link is written as "place + the source's fault + fix", with no "usually means" clause. On tier-A sheets it is kept only where it carries the required warning link (shoulder_press `feel.rows[0]` shoulder).
