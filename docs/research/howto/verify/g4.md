## Calibration

The planted card `single_arm_cable_curl` was a copy of `cable_curl`. The planter made two errors:
1. **c3, a direction flip.** "move only slightly **forward, never back**" became "slightly **back, never forward**". The quote says "keep it still or move it slightly forward". This claim feeds the "Elbows at sides" checkpoint and a tell.
2. **c10, a number change.** ACSM "1-2 s each way" became "1-3 s each way". The quote says "1-2 s CON; 1-2 s ECC". This claim feeds plate.tempo.

Result: **the critic caught 2/2** (curls part 1 critic). Both errors were found by comparing claim to quote, with the exact restoring fix.

Caveat: that critic also noticed the file was untracked and not in census, and called it "almost certainly the planted copy". Both errors were still found on their own merits. No second critic was run, because none was missed. Both plant files are deleted.
