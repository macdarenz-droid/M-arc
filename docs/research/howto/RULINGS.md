# Supervisor rulings on research open items (library How-to)

One entry per ruling, add-only. Verifier notes that raised them are in `verify/<group>.md`.

| Id | Raised by | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| LR-1 | g1 4.1a | assisted_pull_up: the census gains two gaps on the assist machine, `mountStep` (step or foot rails, the way on and off) and `stackPin` (the assistance selector). They are drawn as equipment only. No text claim is added until a source is found. |
| LR-2 | g1 4.1b, 4.2.2 | shoulder_press: the census gains the gap `pivotArmOverhead`, an arc that carries the handles up in front of or beside the head and visibly clear of it. The drawing gate gets a head-clearance check. Until a wide-handle option exists, draw the close handles (c7). Head clearance is solved in the drawing and needs no text claim. |
| LR-3 | g1 4.2.1, g3 4 | A tier-A "way out" (dismount, failing a rep, setting a weight down) is never invented. It stays in `gaps` and no way-out line is shown until a source is found. Tracker item LIB-WAYOUT covers one small fetcher task per equipment type: bar dismount, assist machine, selectorized press, dumbbell or goblet set-down. The owner sheet lists every open tier-A gap. |
| LR-4 | g1 4.2.3 | Where no source names a palm direction, the plate shows the equipment primitive's natural grip (neutral for a single D-handle; the close handles for the shoulder press). It is recorded as a drawing convention in `notes`, never as a claim. |
| LR-5 | g1 4.2.4 | chin_up added load: the sourced order (more reps, less assistance, then added load) is enough. No load amount is given. |
| LR-6 | g1 4.2.5, 4.2.6 | R1 confirmed: no feel row carries an unsourced "stop and get it checked". The shared red-flag box and the owner's disclaimer ("General guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.") cover it. A row with no sourced symptom-to-cause link is written as "place + the source's fault + fix", with no invented "usually means" clause. |
| LR-7 | g3 2 | Parent picks follow the library architecture (sections 6.1/6.2, owner-approved, with pilot A anchors as template parents). The architecture supersedes content.md appendix A's older grouping, so walking_lunge, forward_lunge and bulgarian_split_squat under reverse_lunge, and jump_squat under box_jump, stand. A child must still share its parent's hand and contact archetype (content.md 2.3); bodyweight_lunge and bodyweight_split_squat correctly became full cards. |
| LR-8 | g3 1 | Inherited lines are checked against the parent's verified card on this branch, or, for an approved golden exercise (e.g. barbell_back_squat), against its approved sheet and grip-and-feel research. front_squat's tier-A unrack and walk-out lines are checked against the approved back-squat sheet before its plate is drawn. |
| LR-9 | g3 3 | pistol_squat: remove `abductors` and add `adductors` in the library muscle list, as its sources say. It is applied when the library data is updated (a later LIB card) and recorded there. |
| LR-10 | g3 5 | New hand-pair keys (e.g. front-rack, cross-arm, goblet-hold) are checked against GA's hand-pair list when their plates are drawn. Each new key goes on the owner's device-check list for the five-second test on a real phone. |
| LR-11 | g1 4.3 | Back red-flag box: the fetcher reads the NHS back-pain page in full, and the box is drafted from content.md 5.3 and checked by the critic. It names **no country-specific phone number**, because the app has users outside the UK: emergency triggers say "Get emergency help now". Until the box is stamped, rows keep `redFlag: "back"` as a pending link, and the stamp step fails rather than drops it. |
| LR-12 | g1 4.4 | CARD-V2 is updated: `claim` may be a string, a list or `inherit:<n>`; the tier comes from appendix B; `gaps`, `tierA` and `notes` are optional fields; an unsourced way-out lives only in `gaps`. |
